Alongside collaborators from Imperial College London, King's College London, University College London, Northwestern University and University of Michigan, Niall attended the DevCell Kickoff Workshop hosted at the Nucleus labs by [b.next](https://bnext.bio/){:target="_blank"}. 

The workshop focused on training for encapsulating [Nucelus Cytosol](https://bnext.bio/){:target="_blank"} within synthetic cells for cell-free protein expression. Additionally, the researchers were introduced to [Nucelus](https://bnext.bio/){:target="_blank"}, which is an open-source platform for documentation around synthetic cell development. The researchers from different institutions will collaborate on a project for one year, with the London node focusing on using synthetic cells to detect and respond to bacterial contamination.
